# Crazy Raccoon: Gator Farm Dash: release report (v1.0.0, 2026-11-01)

An original lane-crossing arcade game at the Arkansas Alligator Farm and Petting Zoo in Hot Springs.
- **Content:** 15 campaign levels in 5 zones, plus the endless Moonlight Run with a top-10 table.
- **Mechanics:** six gator behaviours, lure tosses, washing snacks in the springs, tail-ring lives, a streak multiplier, three stars per level, a hidden ball in every level and a Farm Notebook of sourced facts.
- **Platforms:** desktop and touch phones, with keyboard, touch and gamepad input.

## Test results (final build)
- **Node** (`npm run sim`): **42/42** pass. Kinds, items, levels, the solver, endless, determinism and fuzz.
- **Browser** (`node shared/test-harness/run.mjs . --milestone final`, desktop 1280×800 and mobile 390×844): **62/63**.
  - The one failure was the mobile boot taking 2,118 ms against a 2,000 ms budget. Another session's test suites were loading the machine at the time.
  - The same test passed 3 of 3 on rerun.
  - No console errors, and no external requests.
- **`--file-url`:** the offline `file://` boot passes 7/7.
- **Visual review captures:** 15 scenes (plus r11b) at both viewports, every one inspected.
  - Contact sheets are in `screenshots/review-5/` and `screenshots/release/`.
  - Screenshots are git-ignored and regenerated by `--file review`.

Software-raster cost swings 50% or more with machine load. During the final runs, Furious Squirrels (13–17 ms on record) measured 23 ms on this machine. The game now follows that project's practice: the budget is enforced with `--gpu`, and software numbers are logged.

Defects 13, 16 and 21 in `DEV-PLAN.md` record three passes that cut software raster work:
- a pre-scaled sprite cache
- removing a full-screen `soft-light` pass
- baking lane overlays into cached strips and batching about 140 strokes per frame down to about 50

## Known gaps (post-release polish)
From the final judge, in rank order:
1. **Readability (S):** warning badges can still stack in crowded endless rows. A badge solver should space them and pin each to its mouth.
2. **Depth (S):** finish the barn interior: stall dividers, and lamp pools on the hay rows.
3. **Juice (S):** anchor the landing ring at Bandit's feet, and fade the takeoff puffs sooner.
4. **Environment (M):** turn the lane stripes into farm-pen banks (mossy stone, reeds, roots), and restore more noon set dressing.
5. **Depth (M):** stronger 2.5D on day and noon rows and curbs.
6. **Mobile (M):** frame Old Ruthless in portrait. The camera leans toward his head, but most of his body stays off screen.

Also:
- The review shot script still puts Bandit on the start row in the mobile boss and endless shots. This is a capture-script quirk, not a game bug.
- Whippers share the paddler's silhouette apart from their tail bands.

## Harness requests (shared/test-harness)
- **`//index.html` bug:** `run.mjs` built the game URL as `//index.html` when the game sits at the served root. Assets then resolved as a protocol-relative host and 404'd. Patched in this game's copy (line 397); the shared copy needs the same fix.
- **`--gpu` default:** a `--gpu` default (or a per-test opt-in) for 2D-canvas performance tests. Software-raster frame cost is too noisy to enforce on a shared machine.
- **Profiling note:** document that `getImageData`-based profiling needs the page served over http (`file://` taints the canvas).
