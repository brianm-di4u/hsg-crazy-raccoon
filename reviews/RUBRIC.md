# Visual review rubric: Crazy Raccoon (chunky cartoon 2.5D)

The target is a **polished cartoon mobile-game look in the browser**, at the detail level of the best casual
2.5D mobile games: thick readable outlines, soft top-lit gradients, saturated colour, layered depth, squash and
stretch, and juicy feedback. It is set at the Arkansas Alligator Farm in Hot Springs. Score only what the
captures show. Each category is scored 1–10 in 0.5 steps (maximum 80), citing at least two captures by name.

| Category | key | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Characters: Bandit and the gator cast (appeal, consistency, kinds distinct) | `characters` | ≥ 7 |
| Environment: lanes, water, props, backdrops, cohesion of painted pieces | `environment` | ≥ 7 |
| Depth and lighting: 2.5D layering, shadows, submersion, zone lighting (day, noon, barn, night) | `depth` | ≥ 7 |
| Readability: hazards, telegraphs, lanes, safe tiles, items at phone size | `readability` | ≥ 7 |
| UI and HUD: menus, HUD, results, typography, touch targets | `ui` | ≥ 7 |
| Juice and motion (hop pose, FX, feedback visible in stills) | `juice` | ≥ 6.5 |
| Mobile portrait framing | `mobile` | ≥ 7 |
| Authenticity: reads as a gator farm in Hot Springs; nothing implies a real logo or endorsement | `authenticity` | ≥ 6.5 |

## Anchors
- **3:** placeholder shapes; inconsistent styles; hard to tell hazards from scenery.
- **5:** coherent but flat; painted pieces clash with vector pieces; hazards readable only with effort.
- **7:** a shippable casual-game look: one consistent painted style, characters with appeal, hazards and safe
  tiles obvious at a glance on a phone, menus that look designed, clear depth cues.
- **9:** top-tier mobile polish: every frame screenshot-worthy, rich but uncluttered, flawless readability.

## IP gate (not scored)
No resemblance to any commercial game's characters (no birds, pigs, slingshots or famous mascots), and no real
logos or signage. Reviewers report any sighting as a `characters` gap with severity 5.
