# Loop 3 changes (facts only)
Changes since loop 2:
- **Night:** a lighter moonlight tint, then a darkness gradient that is clear around Bandit and deepens to navy away from him. Flashlight pools are feathered ellipses with no rim. Warning badges and bite footprints draw above the darkness at full brightness. Eyeshine is a tight dot on each painted eye (red for the boss).
- **Juice:** nose wakes are two tapered white wedges, snacks get an 8-ray sparkle every 1.2 s, and spring steam blobs are larger. r11 now shows a real mid-hop frame (the test asserts the hop state).
- **Bandit in play:** a new painted three-quarter rear view that shows his mask and an eye, standing and mid-leap.
- **Gators:** every other paddler is a darker, scarred swimmer variant, painted to match.
- **Portrait:** an opaque dark-green header band behind both HUD rows. The start row sits higher, leaving the Lure coin a gutter below the board.
- **Panels:** the gameplay HUD hides under any panel, behind a darker scrim. Tall panels (map, help) show a bouncing "scroll for more" cue until scrolled to the end. The result stars carry goal labels (Den, Snacks, Par).
- **Environment:** desktop margins fade out softly from the board edge with no hard seam. Tall props (pines, lanterns) are kept off rows just below water. Stacked water lanes alternate slightly in tone, and the float rope is heavier.
- **Barn:** a warm interior grade and murky green-teal water. Each heat lamp hangs from a timber crossbeam laid along a row boundary.
- **Noon:** the warm grade is now a plain overlay. The old soft-light pass cost about 12 ms a frame in software rendering.
