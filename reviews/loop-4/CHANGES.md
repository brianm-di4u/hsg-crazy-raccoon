# Loop 4 changes (facts only)
Changes since loop 3:
- **Night:** the moonlit rim pass now draws the sprite only, so gators no longer sit in lighter boxes. The darkness falls off more steeply around Bandit (clear near him, about 70% navy at the edges). Margin lanterns cast warm pools on the ground. The flashlight cone is three nested feathered wedges. Eyeshine sits on the painted eyes, the boss's included.
- **Desktop:** the checkpoint-row highlight now spans the full width, so there is no lighter box with vertical seams. Tall props (pines, lanterns) never stand beside a row with gators or below water.
- **Juice:** speed strokes trail behind the hop direction (below Bandit when he hops up). Wakes are wider wedges with a foam blob at the nose, and steam blobs are larger. New capture **r11b-land**: two frames after Bandit lands on a gator's back, from level 1's solver plan.
- **Portrait:** a fully opaque header band, and a stone curb footer as tall as the Lure coin, which now sits inside it. The board ends above the curb. Warning badges stay inside the screen edge, and gators whose heads are well off screen show none. A pulsing edge marker shows when Old Ruthless is just off screen.
- **Barn:** desktop margins are plank walls with posts and a warm window. Timber beams are thicker. Heat lamps hang only over hay rows, smaller, from the beams.
- **Characters:** Bandit is drawn larger in play (1.3 tiles tall). Hatchlings are drawn larger, and swimmers are flattened a touch so stacked lanes overlap less.
- **New painted art (Codex batch 6):** a bruiser gator variant with its own silhouette (broad snout, notched tail, scarred squint) for every other paddler. A farm signpost (a painted gator and pecan, no letters) on both sides of every start row. A gator-egg incubator box in the nursery margins, and a visitor boardwalk railing in the day and noon margins.
