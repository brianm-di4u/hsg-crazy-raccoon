# Loop 5 changes (facts only)
Changes since loop 4:
- **Barn:** the overhead beams are solid timber only in the margins, with just a faint shadow across the board, so nothing on a lane is hidden. Heat-lamp fixtures hang outside the playfield (desktop margins) or at the screen edge (portrait), and their floor glows follow them.
- **Props:** a tall prop's canopy reaches into the row above, so pines, lanterns, the feeding stage and the railing now stay off any row where gators cross that row or the one above. On phones, a small farm signpost stands at the screen edge of every start row.
- **Readability:** warning badges sit beside the mouth (closer than before) and never under the header. At night, gator backs are lifted (moonlit rim at 46%) so riding surfaces read against the water.
- **Juice:** landing on a gator gives a stronger squash, makes the gator dip under Bandit, and adds a splash of droplets with the ring.
- **Depth:** land rows are top-lit (lighter far edge, darker near edge). Land gators cast a tight contact shadow plus a faint wider one in place of the long oval. Day has a faint cool grade and noon a warm one, so the two read differently.
- **Portrait footer:** the curb is shorter (Lure coin height), with a mossy lip, grass tufts and a dark front face.
- **Captures:** the night, boss and pond shots now place Bandit mid-board on the rows the script requests.
